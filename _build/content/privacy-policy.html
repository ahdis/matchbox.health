1. Collection and processing of personal data

Data we collect and process in order to make the Website and the products available

You may visit the Website and obtain information about ahdis and our products and services without telling us who you are. As with any connection to a web server, the server on which we make available the Website automatically logs and stores certain technical data and stores it for a short time. This includes the IP address and operating system of your device, the date and time of use and the type of browser you use to access the Website. This is necessary for the optimal and secure provision of the Website.

Personal data we collect and process in order to respond to your inquiries

If you contact us using our contact forms or by email or telephone, we collect and process the information you provide to us. This will regularly include personal data such as your name, business email address and telephone number, and information on a potential affiliation with a company you work for or represent.

Personal data we collect and process in order to allow access to and use of the products or services

We collect and process the following types of personal data in order to allow the use of our products: Your organization or company, your name, your email address, your address, your phone number and data, which are necessary for the invoicing and the chosen payment method.

The access and use of certain functionalities of our products may require prior registration. In the registration process, we will ask you to provide personal data such as your IP-address, name and email address.

Use of web analytics

If you agree to it, we use Google Analytics, a service of Google Ireland Limited, to measure and analyse the use of the Website. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects usage data such as the pages you visit and the time you spend on them. Your IP address is shortened before it is stored, and no analytics data is collected unless you have accepted analytics cookies. We use the resulting reports only in aggregate form, to understand which parts of the Website are useful.

2. Note on our use of cookies

We use cookies, tags and similar technologies on the Website to provide you with the best possible user experience and to help us improve the Website. Cookies are text files that are downloaded to your computer or mobile device when you visit a website or use an app.

Some of the cookies we use are necessary in order to make available certain features of the Website or our products. In addition, we use cookies to enable web analytics. Analytics cookies are only set if you agree to them: when you first visit the Website you are asked whether to allow them, and none are set unless you accept. You can change that decision at any time, and you can also set your preferences in your browser.
